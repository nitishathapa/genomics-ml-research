PROJECT AREA
Computational genomics + ML for clinically relevant genomic interpretation

WHY THIS INTERESTS ME
I work in NGS molecular biology and generate large amounts of sequencing data, 
so I am naturally curious about what the variants, genes, and biomarkers in those datasets actually mean. 
I am especially interested in making genomic interpretation easier for molecular biologists 
who may not have deep bioinformatics expertise. Long term, I would like to explore whether computational or 
machine learning methods can help build a platform that allows scientists to investigate clinically relevant variants,
genes, and biomarkers with less dependence on a specialized SME for every question.

LONG-TERM VISION
Build a platform that helps molecular biologists investigate and interpret clinically relevant genomic variants,
genes, and biomarkers more independently.

CURRENT RESEARCH QUESTION
Can computational and machine learning methods help prioritize and contextualize clinically relevant genomic variants, 
genes, or biomarkers in a way that is accurate, interpretable, and accessible to molecular biology researchers?

POSSIBLE FIRST PROJECT
Start with one narrow problem:
- variant prioritization
- gene prioritization
- biomarker relevance
- evidence interpretation

THINGS TO RESEARCH NEXT
- what tools already exist
- where current tools fail
- what public datasets exist
- what can be measured objectively
- which problem is feasible for one semester
